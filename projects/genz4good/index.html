CONTEXT

Background

Charities face declining long-term donor engagement and growing distrust, particularly among younger generations. While Gen Z shows strong interest in social impact, traditional donation models fail to resonate with their behaviours and expectations.

Key Question

How might charities shift from transactional fundraising to relationship-based engagement that nurtures future giving?
